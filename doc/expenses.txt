Travel Expenses
Hotel costs are reimbursed up to $180 per night.
Flights must be booked in economy class.
Mileage is reimbursed at $0.65 per mile for personal car use.

Meal Expenses
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $40 per day.
Alcohol is not reimbursed.

Submitting Receipts
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
Expenses over $25 require an itemized receipt.
Late submissions may not be reimbursed.
