Paid Time Off (PTO)
New employees receive 15 PTO days per year.
After 3 years of service, employees receive 20 PTO days per year.
Up to 5 unused PTO days can roll over to the next year.

Sick Leave
All employees receive 8 sick days per year.
Sick days do not roll over to the next year.
A doctor's note is required for more than 3 sick days in a row.

Holidays
The company observes 10 paid holidays per year.
The holiday schedule is posted on the HR portal every January.

Requesting Time Off
PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
Requests are approved by your direct manager.
