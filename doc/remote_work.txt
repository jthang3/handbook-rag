Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
Remote days must be on Monday or Friday.
New employees must work in the office for their first 90 days.

Core Hours
All employees must be available between 10 AM and 3 PM Eastern.
Meetings should be scheduled during core hours when possible.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and one monitor for home use.
Employees can request a $200 one-time stipend for a home office chair.
